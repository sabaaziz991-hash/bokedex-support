Privacy Policy

How Bokedex handles app data and permissions.

Overview Support Privacy Terms
On-device first

Bokedex is designed to keep analysis on the device.

The app currently does not include analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or a remote account system for the main dog analysis features.

What the app may access

  • Camera: when you capture a dog photo or use a camera-based feature.
  • Photo library: when you choose an existing dog photo for analysis.
  • Microphone: when you record a bark clip for local audio analysis.

How data is processed

  • Dog photos, camera frames, and bark clips are processed locally on your iPhone.
  • Local coaching records and related app data are stored on your device.
  • The app does not currently upload this analysis data to a Bokedex server for scoring.

Control and retention

  • You can remove the app and its local data from your device at any time.
  • You can revoke camera, microphone, or photo-library access in iOS Settings.
