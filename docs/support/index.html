Before you email

What to include

  • Your iPhone model.
  • Your iOS version.
  • The Bokedex app version.
  • The feature or screen you were using.
  • A short description of what happened.

Common checks

  • Camera or microphone issue: confirm the permission is enabled for Bokedex in iOS Settings.
  • Photo import issue: confirm the selected image is stored locally on the device.
  • Unexpected read: try a clearer dog photo or a bark clip with less background overlap.
